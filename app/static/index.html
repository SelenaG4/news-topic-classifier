How this works

Every headline below gets classified into one of four AG News topics — World, Sports, Business, or Sci/Tech — by two different models: a classical baseline (TF-IDF features + logistic regression, trained fresh on real data when this service starts) and a fine-tuned DistilBERT transformer. Both are shown side by side so the transformer's accuracy has to earn its extra complexity against a real number, not an assumed one.
